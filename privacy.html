G-Sync — Privacy policy

G-Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What data is accessed

With the drive.file scope, G-Sync can access only the Google Drive files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see or read any other files in Google Drive.

How data is used

G-Sync stores the owner's own Garmin Connect health data in those files for personal analysis. All processing happens on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or sent to any server other than Google Drive and Garmin Connect.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
